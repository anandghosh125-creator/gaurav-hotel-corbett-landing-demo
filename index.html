Our Philosophy

Slow Living on the Edge of India’s Oldest Tiger Reserve

Built with sun-dried Kumaoni mud plaster, reclaimed teak, and river stone, Aranya Corbett is designed to breathe with the forest rather than stand apart from it.

Just 12 minutes from the Bijrani Safari Gate, our working permaculture farm grows heirloom grains, lemongrass, turmeric, and mango orchards. Every stay directly supports local village artisans and wildlife corridor conservation.

Curated Days

Wilderness & Farm Experiences

From dawn Gypsy safaris in Dhikala to slow afternoons harvesting organic vegetables with our chefs.

Dawn & Dusk Tiger Safaris

Our resident naturalists arrange 4x4 Gypsy permits across Corbett’s premier zones—Dhikala grasslands, Bijrani’s dense Sal canopies, and Jhirna’s rocky riverbeds.

  • Dhikala Zone: 45 min drive • Iconic grasslands & Ramganga reservoir
  • Bijrani Zone: 12 min drive • High Bengal tiger & elephant density
  • Jhirna & Dhela: 25 min drive • Year-round open wilderness

Kumaoni Slow-Fire Kitchen

Ninety percent of our produce travels less than 100 meters from soil to plate. Enjoy wood-fired clay-pot curries, millet rotis, and wild honey harvested from our apiary.

  • Morning Harvest Walk: Pick herbs & greens with our head chef
  • Orchard High Tea: Pahadi masala chai & warm mandua cookies
  • Starlit Bonfire Dinner: Slow-cooked Kumaoni thali under the Sal trees

600+ Bird Species & Kosi Trails

Walk quietly along the Kosi River boulders at sunrise with binoculars in hand. Spot Great Hornbills, Crested Kingfishers, and Himalayan Rubythroats before breakfast.

  • Guided Birding Walk: 6:00 AM daily with Swarovski optics
  • Kosi River Picnic: Barefoot tea by clear mountain streams
